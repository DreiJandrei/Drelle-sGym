FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime with no penalty. Annual plans run for the full year and won't renew if you cancel before the renewal date.

Is there a joining fee?

No. The joining fee is waived on every plan, so you only pay for your membership.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade or downgrade from your member pass at any time. Your member ID stays the same.

What's included in the free personal training session?

A one-on-one session with a certified coach: a fitness assessment, goal setting and a starter program built around you.

Can I bring a friend?

Every plan includes guest passes each month: 1 on Basic, 2 on Premium and 5 on Elite.

Ready to start?

Your first personal training session is on us. Join online in minutes, or drop by the club for a free tour.
